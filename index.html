Louisiana · 85 miles along the Mississippi

Cancer Alley

Nearly 200 fossil-fuel and petrochemical facilities line this stretch of river between Baton Rouge and New Orleans. Their pollution reaches neighboring communities through the air, water, and soil — in one area, the cancer risk from industrial air pollution is more than seven times the national average.
